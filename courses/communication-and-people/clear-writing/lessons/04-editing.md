Good writing is rewriting. This lesson gives you the structure to aim for and a checklist to get there.

## Sentence length and rhythm

Short sentences hit hard. Longer sentences let ideas unfold, connect causes to effects, and carry the reader along a line of reasoning that would feel choppy if chopped up. Mix them. A run of short sentences feels breathless. A run of long ones feels like wading. Vary the length and the writing breathes.

Rule of thumb: average under 20 words. Anything over 35, split.

## Paragraphs

-   One idea per paragraph.
-   First sentence states the idea. The rest supports it.
-   A reader skimming only first sentences should still follow your argument.
-   Three to six sentences is a comfortable range. One-sentence paragraphs are fine for emphasis.

## Signposts

Headings, numbered lists, and bold key phrases let readers navigate. In anything longer than a page, use them. But don't bold whole sentences or use six levels of heading. Signposts work because they're rare.

## Transitions

Words like "but", "so", "because", "for example", and "however" show how ideas relate. Without them, readers have to infer the logic. "Sales fell. We cut prices." versus "Sales fell, so we cut prices." The second tells you the cause.

## The editing checklist

Run every draft through this, in order:

1.  **Point first?** Does the opening sentence say what matters most?
2.  **Cut 20%.** Delete padding, nominalisations, needless passives, intensifiers.
3.  **Concrete?** Replace abstractions with numbers, names, examples.
4.  **Sentences.** Split any over 35 words. Vary rhythm.
5.  **Paragraphs.** One idea each, first sentence carries it.
6.  **Read it aloud.** Anywhere you stumble, the reader will too. Fix it.
7.  **Sleep on it** if you can. Fresh eyes catch what tired ones miss.

## A complete before-and-after

> **Before:** As per our previous communications, it has come to our attention that there have been a number of instances where deliveries have not been made in a timely manner, and as a result of this we are in the process of reviewing our logistics arrangements in order to ensure that improvements can be implemented going forward.
> **After:** Several deliveries were late last month. We're reviewing our logistics and will have a fix in place by 1 June.
