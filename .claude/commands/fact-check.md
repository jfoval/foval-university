---
description: Adversarial fact-check of one lesson (can be run alone)
argument-hint: <path/to/lesson.md>
---
Read `docs/EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md` Part 2, then the lesson at `$ARGUMENTS` and its course's `research/SOURCES.md`.

Assume the lesson contains errors and find them. Build a table of every checkable claim (numbers, dates, names, quotes, mechanisms, legal/medical/financial facts). For each: verify against SOURCES.md or fetch a primary or standard source on the web; record verdict (verified / wrong / unverifiable / needs citation) and the evidence. Then fix the lesson: correct errors, add citations to a `## Sources` section, reword unverifiable claims as uncertain or remove them. Append findings to the course's `research/REVIEWS.md`. Report the table and what changed.
